# Nearby Sequences Have the Same Limit

If two real sequences \((x_n)\) and \((y_n)\) satisfy

\[
x_n\longrightarrow x,
\qquad
|x_n-y_n|\longrightarrow0,
\]
then \(y_n\to x\).

By the triangle inequality,

\[
|y_n-x|\le |y_n-x_n|+|x_n-x|.
\]
Both terms on the right converge to \(0\), so the left-hand side also converges to \(0\).
Therefore \(y_n\to x\).
